Executive reporting / Power BI

PitchSide Pro

A five-page Power BI report designed to help an executive audience understand revenue growth, customer behavior, and commercial priorities.

View on GitHub ↗Explore live report ↗
Project typeIndependent portfolio project
AudienceExecutive leadership
ToolsPower BI · Power Query · DAX

The question

What drives revenue change, and where should the business focus next?

PitchSide Pro is a fictional football merchandise retailer. The report examines whether football events explain growth, then follows the evidence through revenue mix, margins, repeat buying, and conversion.

How I built it

  • Prepared and modeled the contest data with Power Query.
  • Created DAX measures for time comparisons, event analysis, and customer cohorts.
  • Structured the report around one executive question on each page.
  • Connected the findings to recommendations on conversion, loyalty, and product mix.

The outcome

The analysis identified conversion as a priority alongside revenue concentration. The final page turns the preceding findings into specific areas for business attention.
